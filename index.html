Rules & Eligibility

👥

Team Size

Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. You can go solo or party up to face the Ender Dragon (aka the deadline) together!

🎓

Eligibility

Open to all students, professionals, and coding enthusiasts. If you can type code, you're welcome here!

⏱️

Fresh Code

All projects must be started from scratch during the hackathon. No pre-existing code allowed (except boilerplate and open-source libraries).

⚖️

Code of Conduct

Be respectful, collaborate effectively, and don't grief other teams' workspaces. Play fair!
